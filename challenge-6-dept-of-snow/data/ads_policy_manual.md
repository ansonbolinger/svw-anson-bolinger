# Alaska Department of Snow (ADS) - Operations Manual & Emergency Directives

## 1. Agency Overview & Operational Priority Matrix
The Alaska Department of Snow (ADS) oversees winter road maintenance, highway safety, avalanche mitigation, and heavy plow dispatch across 16,000+ lane-miles of Alaska highways and mountain passes.

### Priority Level Classifications:
- **Priority 1 (Critical Corridors):** Seward Highway (AK-1), Glenn Highway (AK-1), Parks Highway (AK-3), Richardson Highway (AK-4). Service standard: continuous 24/7 clearing during storm events. Maximum snow accumulation before plow intervention: 1.5 inches.
- **Priority 2 (Regional Arterials):** Sterling Highway (AK-1 south of Tern Lake), Haines Highway, Dalton Highway (industrial convoy lanes). Service standard: cleared within 6 hours of storm cessation.
- **Priority 3 (Rural & Feeder Connectors):** Local access roads and scenic byways. Service standard: cleared within 24 hours of storm cessation.

## 2. Severe Storm Protocols & Road Closure Thresholds

### 2.1 Pass Closure Directives:
- **Thompson Pass (Richardson Hwy MP 12-42):** Mandatory closure when sustained winds exceed 45 mph with visibility under 50 feet (blizzard conditions), or when Avalanche Hazard Index is rated "EXTREME".
- **Turnagain Pass (Seward Hwy MP 57-75):** Immediate closure upon active avalanche release reaching road surface. Chains mandatory for all commercial vehicles exceeding 10,000 lbs GVWR when "Code Black" is declared.
- **Hatcher Pass:** Winter seasonal gate closure in effect from October 1 to May 15.

### 2.2 Avalanche Hazard Ratings & Actions:
- **Low:** Normal plow operations.
- **Moderate:** Spotter required for rotary blower operations.
- **High:** Maintenance operations restricted to daylight hours; pass pilot-car convoys only.
- **Extreme:** Complete corridor shutdown. Mandatory evacuation of non-essential personnel.

## 3. Plow Fleet Deployment & Equipment Specifications

### Fleet Unit Designations:
- **Alpha Series (Tandem-Axle Heavy V-Plows):** Equipped with 14-foot reversible steel cutting edges and dual wing plows. Operating speed: 25-35 mph. Used for high-volume expressway clearing.
- **Bravo Series (Rotary Snow Blowers - Kodiak/Oshkosh):** Displacement capacity: 3,000 to 5,000 tons/hour. Deployed exclusively for avalanche debris clearing and drift removal exceeding 4 feet depth.
- **Charlie Series (Anti-Icing Chemical Spreaders):** Disperses Magnesium Chloride (MgCl2) brine pre-treatment at 35 gallons/lane-mile for pavement temps down to -10°F (-23°C); dry salt/sand grit abrasive mix below -10°F.

## 4. Public Communications & Emergency Broadcast Guidelines
- All road status advisories and travel alerts must be categorized into:
  - **GREEN (NORMAL):** Pavements wet/plowed; normal winter driving precautions.
  - **AMBER (DIFFICULT):** Reduced speeds, packed snow, freezing fog, blowing snow. Commercial chains advised.
  - **RED (HAZARDOUS/CLOSED):** Travel strongly discouraged or prohibited. Emergency convoys only.
- Public broadcast statements must include: Affected Highway, Milepost Range, Hazard Description, and Expected Clearance Window.
